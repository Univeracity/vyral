# Code of Conduct

Contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate constructively, respect differing
experience levels, focus critique on ideas and code, and protect sensitive information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and publication of another person's private
information are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove content or limit participation when needed
to keep the project safe and productive.

Report conduct concerns privately through the security-advisory channel in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are handled discreetly to the extent practical.
